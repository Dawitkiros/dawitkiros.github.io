I am a PhD researcher in the [Signal Processing Group at NTNU](https://www.ntnu.edu/ies/signal-processing-group) and a visiting PhD researcher at [CISPA](https://cispa.de/).
My research combines machine learning algorithm design, convergence analysis, and reproducible PyTorch experiments, with a focus on communication-efficient federated learning and distributed optimization under client heterogeneity and partial participation.
My applied experience includes medical image segmentation and data engineering with PySpark, Snowflake, and PostgreSQL.
